Presenter controls

→ / Space / tap — next reveal: every tap steps ONE beat, so a staged slide gives up its next reveal before the show moves on  ·  ← / tap the far left — one beat back
1–7 — jump to a night  ·  O — all slides at a glance  ·  D — director mode (auto-advance; nominee slides still wait for you)  ·  F — fullscreen  ·  M — sound on/off  ·  H — this help
The control bars stay hidden while you present — they never pop up when you tap. Summon them only on purpose: press H, tap-and-hold anywhere on the stage, or tap the stage’s top-right corner. They fade again on their own.
Nominee slides are your beat: the room guesses, then advance to crown the winner.
Sound comes from the app's own library, played from this site. The first tap or keypress wakes the audio.
This page reads the live scores straight from the same database the scoring app writes to — nothing to regenerate. Tap ⬇ DOWNLOAD SHOW (top bar) to save a fully offline copy of tonight's show.
